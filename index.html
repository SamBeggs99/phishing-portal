Do this today — 5 minutes, right now

The single most effective thing training can do is lead to immediate action.

01 · MFA

Check that Multi-Factor Authentication is enabled on your Microsoft 365 account. If it isn't, contact IT today.

02 · Lock screen

Press Win+L (Windows) or Cmd+Ctrl+Q (Mac) right now to lock your screen. Practice the habit.

03 · Password check

Is your work password unique — not used anywhere else? If not, change it now and consider a password manager.

04 · Report button

Find the "Barracuda Outlook Add-In" button in your Outlook now, before you need it. It's in the Home ribbon or right-click menu.

05 · Desk check

Look at your desk right now. Any passwords written down? Any sensitive documents visible? Clear them before you leave today.
